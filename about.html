Hi there! I'm Feiry.

I'm a Filipino-Canadian Junior Graphic Designer & Illustrator based in rain-soaked, scenic Vancouver, BC. My path into design started with a simple fascination: how colors and shapes evoke real emotion. That curiosity carried me through Media Arts and Video Game Design before landing squarely in Graphic Design and Illustration. Today, I combine these disciplines to transform individual and community visions into vibrant, high-impact visuals.

What I Do

I craft design assets that bridge creativity with strategy, including:

  • Visual Identity & Branding Systems
  • Logo Design, Ads & Publications
  • Visual Storytelling & Custom Digital Illustration

My Approach

Great design is a mirror—it reflects the heart, personality, and values of the person or brand behind it. Rather than leaning on a single aesthetic, I pull from a wide spectrum of influences to build identities that feel fresh, innovative, and deeply human-centric.

When I'm Out of the Office...

You’ll usually find me hunting down new food spots, browsing the aisles at Chapters, discovering new music, exploring local museums, or hitting up art conventions!

Let’s build something whimsical together!
