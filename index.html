Hi, I'm Feiry!

I’m a Graphic Designer & Illustrator, I help early-stage brands and growing businesses stand out with visual identity systems, publications, and custom digital artwork. Creative Services

Visual Storytelling Design

Human-centric visuals. Multidimentsional stories.

I help individuals and brands to tell their unique stories into vibrant and expressive digital illustrations. By weaving together diverse influences and modern design trends, I create visual systems that feel human, memorable, and deeply aligned with your values. Whether the goal is to humanize a startup or breathe life into a publication, my focus is on emotional resonance and visual innovation.

What I Offer

  • Mascot & Character Design: Developing a relatable face for your brand that embodies your personality and connects with your audience.
  • Brand Illustration Systems: A cohesive library of bespoke icons and assets that ensure your brand’s visual story is consistent across all platforms.
  • Book Cover Design: Capturing the heart of a story through a single, high-impact image that commands attention on a shelf or a screen.
  • Editorial & Web Graphics: Dynamic visuals for apps, magazines, and websites that transform static content into an immersive narrative.


Visual Identity Design

A visual soul for your brand.

By weaving together multidimensional influences, I translate your vision into a cohesive, expressive visual system that feels human, vibrant, and undeniably yours.

  • Personality-Based Colour Palettes: A curated selection of vibrant tones that trigger the right emotional responses.
